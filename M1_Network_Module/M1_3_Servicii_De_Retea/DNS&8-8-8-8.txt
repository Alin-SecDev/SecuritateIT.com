What is DNS and 8.8.8.8

DNS - Domain Name Service - Think of the DNS as the phonebook of the Internet. You provide a name and DNS finds the corresponding address. 

The most common DNS are:
8.8.8.8 or 8.8.4.4 this is from Google. 
1.1.1.1 or 1.0.0.1 this is from Cloudflare.
9.9.9.9 or 149.112.112.112 this is from Quad9
